## What each scenario does

| scenario | effect on the system | basis |
|---|---|---|
| bus_flood_dos | A node on the data bus floods both channels so navigation messages are not delivered | assumption: one attempt per 2000 h; on-board monitoring notices loss of traffic in 15 min and isolating the node takes 1 h |
| gnss_spoofing | False GNSS signals make both navigation units compute a wrong position | assumption: one episode per 5000 h; miss probability 0.1 as in CPS-02; a missed spoof is found in 30 min and re-validating navigation takes 2 h; RF-domain effect with no ATT&CK for ICS technique |
| false_nav_injection | False navigation messages are injected on the data bus from a compromised bus device | assumption: one episode per 10000 h; plausibility checks reject half of the injections at onset; detection 1 h and re-validation 2 h |
| message_delay_replay | Navigation messages are held back or replayed so they arrive 80 ms late against a 50 ms deadline | assumption: one episode per 3300 h; latency and deadline illustrative; T0830 is the closest technique (an adversary in the middle can block or modify traffic) |
